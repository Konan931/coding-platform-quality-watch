# Reporting Workflow

## Minimum report

A useful report answers:

1. What platform and feature were tested?
2. What was expected?
3. What was observed?
4. Under which environment?
5. How can another person reproduce it?
6. What evidence is available?
7. How confident are we?
8. What is the current lifecycle state?

## Review rule

Do not convert an observation into a platform-wide conclusion without evidence that supports the broader scope.

## Positive findings

Reports may also document strong teaching patterns, robust autograders, clear error messages, useful accessibility features, or exemplary sandbox behavior.

## Safety and privacy

Do not include:

- passwords, API keys, session cookies, or bearer tokens;
- private learner data;
- unnecessary account identifiers;
- evidence that exposes another person's private information.

Redact before committing.
